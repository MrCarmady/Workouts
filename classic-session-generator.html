How this is calculated

  • Pace base. The same model as the Race Prediction Calculator: Riegel (time × distance ratio ^ 1.06), a slow-down for results under 5km, and a marathon slow-down that depends on weekly distance. Paces are named for the race distance they match, such as 5k pace: the predicted race pace over that distance. M pace is marathon pace.
  • Tempo. 20 minutes runs at threshold, the predicted 16km pace. Longer tempos slow down by 4.9% × ln(minutes ÷ 20), never slower than marathon pace, up to 60 minutes. The scale is a fit to a Daniels-style table you supplied (+1.2% at 25 minutes, +5.3% at 60). I have not seen the source of that table.
  • Ladder. The 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 minute reps run at 10k, 5k, 3k, 2k and 1500m pace. The jog between reps is 90s by default.
  • Aussie quarters. 400m at 4k pace, then 200m floated at M pace to 3% faster, repeated with no stop. Eight reps is 4.8km, 5km with an opening 200m float.
  • Mona fartlek. 2×90s, 4×60s, 4×30s and 4×15s, each followed by a float of the same length, as in Nate Jenkins' article on the Moneghetti fartlek. Efforts run at 5k, 4k, 3k and mile pace. The article says about 5k effort for the 90s, getting faster as reps shorten. Floats run at 85% of 5km speed (my choice), about 5s/km slower than marathon pace for an 18:24 runner. The article calls them a quick jog.
  • Yasso 800s. 800m at 5k pace with a jog of the same time. The old rule (800m time in minutes:seconds = marathon time in hours:minutes) is shown for comparison only.
  • Rosario 800s. 800m at 5k pace with an 800m float at 5k pace plus 25s per 800m, which is about +31s/km.
  • 1km repeats, mile repeats, 30/30. 1km at 4.5k pace, 1 mile at 10k pace, and 30s at 3k pace with a 30s jog.
  • Marathon-pace long run. Easy running first, then the chosen distance at marathon pace.
  • Session load. TSS = hours × (threshold pace ÷ pace)² × 100. Warm-up, cool-down and jogs run at 62.5% of 5km speed unless you change the pace.
  • The paces are my choices from your descriptions, not published prescriptions. This page does not apply weekly-volume limits. Check the numbers against heart rate or feel.
