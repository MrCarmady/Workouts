    How this works

    Marathon time: from your result, using the same model as the Race Prediction Calculator (Riegel exponent 1.06, short-race adjustment, and a marathon slow-down set by weekly distance; 70 miles a week is assumed if the box is empty). If your result is a marathon, that time is used as entered.

    Verdict: your marathon time divided by the time needed. Up to 0.97 is "Probably", up to 1.06 is "Maybe", above that "Unlikely". These cut-offs are my own placeholders, not from any race or study. A prediction from a shorter race assumes marathon-specific training; a first marathon is often slower than predicted.

    Needed: the published standard, except Boston, where entry is fastest-first and I subtract the most recent cut-off margin I found (5:17 for 2027, from a headline only; the 2026 margin was 4:34). Other races that fill fastest-first (London, Sydney) also have margins that I do not have, so a standard is not a guarantee of a place.

    Standards were read from running-news summaries and the London Marathon page, not from every race's own site. They change each year. Check the race's page before relying on them. Left out: Sydney (two sources give different tables), New York for women (sources differ by 12 minutes at 18 to 34), and elite and championship routes.
