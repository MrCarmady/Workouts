How this is calculated

Training stress for each part is hours × (threshold pace ÷ average pace)² × 100. The warm-up and cool-down score is worked out from the pace you enter. What remains of the target is assigned to the workout efforts, and their pace is solved from that. Enter total minutes at effort pace, not including recoveries.

If you enter a race instead of a threshold pace, the race is converted to a 5km equivalent with Riegel's formula (time × distance ratio ^ 1.06). A result shorter than 5km is slowed by 8s/km × ln(5 ÷ distance in km) (about 9s/km for the mile). Threshold pace is then taken as the pace for 16km, using the same formula. These are estimates.

Plans are rejected when the warm-up and cool-down alone already reach the target, when the required effort pace is outside 2:00–15:00 per km, or when the whole session works out to more than 120 TSS per hour.
