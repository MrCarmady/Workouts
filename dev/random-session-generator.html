How this is calculated

  • The draw. A coin flip picks the family, Classic or Sub-Threshold. Then every session in that family that fits the time has the same chance, and one version of it is picked at random. There is no filter for recent training, hard days or weekly TSS.
  • Classic sessions. Tempo, Rowland Tempo, ladder, Aussie quarters, 1 min I / 1 min MP, Pre's 200s, Mona fartlek, Yasso 800s, Rosario 800s, 1km repeats, mile repeats, 30/30 and the marathon-pace long run. Structure, paces and notes are the Classic Session Generator's, with its default options and a range of rep counts.
  • Sub-Threshold sessions. Reps of 200, 300 or 400m, or 3, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15 or 20 minutes. Paces, recoveries and rep ranges are the Sub-Threshold Session Generator's. The three short distances run faster than sub-threshold, and are named by their distance. In a short slot the rep count can drop to half the usual low end.
  • Fitting the time. The whole session, warm-up and cool-down included, must run between 70% and 100% of the time you pick. Warm-up and cool-down are 10 + 10 minutes for 60 and 90 minutes, 8 + 8 for 45 and 5 + 5 for 30 (my choice). The marathon-pace long run has none.
  • Pace base. The same model as the Race Prediction Calculator. Marathon pace assumes 70 miles a week.
  • Session load. TSS = hours × (threshold pace ÷ pace)² × 100. Warm-up, cool-down and jogs run at 65% of 5km speed; sub-threshold recoveries are walked at 10:00/km.
