Notes

  • Shorthand. A number before E, M or T is miles (4M = 4 miles at marathon pace). I and R reps are timed. Jogs and easy segments are at easy pace and count towards the distance.
  • Paces. Marathon pace is the Riegel prediction. Threshold is race(17), intervals race(4), repetition race(1.7). Easy is a cap at 65% of 5km speed, not a target.
  • Weekly total. Peak distance times the week's fraction of peak. Easy days share what is left after the two quality sessions.
  • Load. TSS = hours × (threshold pace ÷ pace)² × 100, with threshold as race(16). Standing rests score 0. CTL has a 42-day and ATL a 7-day time constant. Q2 falls on Wednesday and Q1 on Sunday, so day order affects ATL slightly. Ranges (steady runs) use the midpoint. Start CTL, if blank, is week 1's TSS divided by 7. Race day itself is not scored.
  • Slower runners. Daniels treats 5 to 6 min of threshold as 1 mile, 10 to 15 min as 2 miles and 16 to 20 min as 3 miles. With the box ticked, threshold segments are capped at 6, 15 and 20 min.
