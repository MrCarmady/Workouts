# Short-Distance Plan Viewer: methodology

The page lays out three plans for 5km and 10km racing from a race result, with paces and load: Daniels' 5K to 10K plan for 40 to 50 miles a week, his plan for 60 to 70 miles a week, and Davis's Full-Spectrum 10k schedule. The race, time, units and starting CTL are shared. Load (TSS, CTL, ATL, ramp) is calculated as on the Marathon Plan Viewer: TSS = hours x (threshold pace / pace)^2 x 100 with threshold as race(16), CTL with a 42-day and ATL with a 7-day time constant, and a blank starting CTL taken as week 1's TSS divided by 7.

The race on the last day is scored at its predicted pace and added to the final week's TSS, time and distance. CTL and ATL for that week are for the morning of the race, and the page gives the values after the race as well.

## Daniels 5K to 10K plans

Tables 11.1 and 11.2 of Daniels' Running Formula, from screenshots of the Kindle edition. The tables are listed in full below so that the transcription can be checked against the book.

### What is included

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Phases II, III and IV (6 weeks each, 18 weeks) | As printed |
| Phase I | Not included. The screenshots start at Phase II. |
| Weekly distance | The book gives one range per table (40 to 50 or 60 to 70 miles). Every week uses the distance you enter, or the middle of the range (45 or 65 miles) if the box is blank. |
| Long run (L) | Lesser of 25% of the week's distance and 120 minutes at easy pace. In a Phase IV race week from week 3 on, the book cuts it to 90 minutes. |
| Easy days | Share the distance left after the long run, quality sessions and race. Strides (ST) are listed and not scored. |
| Phase IV | The book prints a race-week and a no-race version of the Tuesday and Friday sessions and of Saturday. The race option on the page chooses which version is used: a race on the last Saturday (default), none, or every Saturday of Phase IV. |
| Phase IV week 6 | Only the race version of Tuesday is printed. It is used with or without a race. |

### Paces

The paces are those of the Marathon Plan Viewer's Daniels plans: easy 74% of 5km speed as written (or your own easy-pace setting), M the Riegel marathon prediction, T = race(17), I = race(4), R = race(1.7). The slower-runner option caps T reps at 6, 15 and 20 minutes for 1, 2 and 3 miles, as on that page.

H (hard) is effort-based. The author read the book's description as a pace for a 10 to 12 minute effort, meant for cases such as training at altitude; the screenshots do not define it and the book gives no pace formula. The page uses I pace for H, as the author decided on 2026-11-12. The H pace box offers halfway between T and I instead.

### Notation

A number before E, M or T is miles. Reps are metres, miles or minutes at the pace of the letter. Jogs run at easy pace and count towards distance and TSS. Rests are standing and score 0. "1K" is 1000m.

### Assumptions where the pages are ambiguous

- 4 to 6 x 1,200m I (Table 11.2, Phase III week 4) is set to 5 reps, and 5 to 8 x 1K I (Phase III week 2) to 6, both within the book's 24-minute limit.
- Where a Phase IV race-week session ends without an easy segment (the printed no-race version has "+ 2 E" or "+ 1 E"), the same easy mileage is used for the race-week version.
- Phase IV week 2, Table 11.2, no-race Tuesday: read as steady 3T + 2E + 2T + 2E.
- Races carry no extra warm-up or cool-down.
- 3 x 2T + 2 min rests (Table 11.2, Phase III week 4, Saturday) is read as 3 reps of 2 miles with 2 minutes' rest.

### Full transcription (Sunday to Saturday)

ST is strides, jg is jog. "(race week); (no race)" gives the two printed versions.

## Davis Full-Spectrum 10k

The 16-week schedule from RunningWritings (percentage-based 10k training, metric PDF). Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Percentages are of 10km speed, so a higher percentage is faster.

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Sessions and distances | As printed, in kilometres |
| Effort words | Read at the midpoints of the schedule's bands: very easy 50 to 55%, easy 55 to 65%, moderate 70 to 80%, strong 85 to 90% |
| Easy runs | 60% unless the schedule gives another percentage; ranges use the midpoint, optional runs included |
| Warm-up and cool-down | Not listed in the schedule but inside its stated weekly volume. The page adds 5 km combined to most sessions, 1.5 to 3 km to some, and none to progression runs and the race (the PDF suggests 2 to 3 km each). |
| Walk recoveries | 6 km/h |
| Walk/jog recoveries | 550 s/km, between walking and jogging. This is an assumption. |
| Jogs | 50% of 10km speed |
| Progression runs | Equal thirds at 60%, 75% and 90%. The PDF gives no definition, so this is an assumption. |
| Race | 10 km at 100% of 10km speed on the last Saturday of week 16 |

The weekly distance the page adds up is shown against the volume the schedule states. At 18:52 for 5km the two agree to within a few kilometres in most weeks (89.1 against 89 in week 1).

The easy-pace setting does not apply to this plan, which sets easy pace as a percentage of 10km speed.
