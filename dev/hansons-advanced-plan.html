Notes

  • Mileage. Distances and weekly totals are the book's. Totals include a 1.5-mile warm-up and cooldown on speed, strength and tempo days. The detail adds up to within a mile or two of the printed total.
  • Paces. Marathon pace is the goal pace: the goal time if entered, otherwise the Race Prediction Calculator's marathon (70 miles a week). Tempo is marathon pace, strength is 10 s/mile faster, and speed is 5km pace. Easy and recovery jogs are at 65% of 5km speed, as a cap.
  • Load. TSS = hours × (threshold pace ÷ pace)² × 100, with threshold as race(16). CTL has a 42-day and ATL a 7-day time constant. Start CTL, if blank, is week 1's TSS divided by 7. The marathon on the last Sunday is not scored, so week 18 is Monday to Saturday and CTL and ATL are for the morning of the race.
