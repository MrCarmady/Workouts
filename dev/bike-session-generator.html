How this is calculated

Powers are percentages of your FTP, the power you can hold for about an hour. The endurance (56 to 75%), threshold (91 to 105%) and VO2max (106 to 120%) bands are Coggan's power levels as I recall them; I have not checked them against the source here. Sweet spot at 88 to 94% is the common coaching convention. The ranges used for each set (for example 98 to 105% for 10-minute threshold reps, lower for longer ones) are my choice. All are conventions, not measurements of you, so adjust to how the session feels.

Sessions and set sizes are my selection of common ones, not a plan from a specific book. Recoveries between reps are easy spinning at 55% of FTP (50% for VO2max). For sweet spot, threshold and over-unders the recovery is about a third of the rep length, rounded to a whole minute (my placeholder); for VO2max it equals the work.

TSS = hours × (power ÷ FTP)² × 100, summed over each block at the middle of its range. This uses average power per block, so it is a little below a normalised-power figure for the variable blocks. Counts towards CTL: the raw TSS times a percentage, 80 by default, as in Cross-Training TSS (Copeland: cycling needs 20 to 30% more time than running for similar aerobic benefit).

Not adjusted for: heat, hills, fatigue, cadence, indoor versus outdoor.
