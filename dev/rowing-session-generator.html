How this is calculated

Every target is a split per 500m set relative to your 2k split, so the session scales with your fitness. Watts come from the Concept2 formula, 2.80 ÷ (split in seconds per metre)³. Threshold is your 2k split plus 15 seconds, as in Cross-Training TSS (Paul's law: add 5 seconds per 500m for each doubling of distance, so 16k is 2k + 15, about an hour for most rowers).

The offsets for each session type (steady state 2k + 22 to 28 s, threshold 2k + 13 to 18 s depending on rep length, race pace 2k − 2 to + 4 s depending on rep length, 5k pace 2k + 6 to + 8.5 s, pyramids 2k + 1 to + 6 s), the set sizes and the rests are my selection, not from a specific programme, and are not checked against a source. Adjust to how the session feels. Rests are light rowing at 2k + 35 s: a third of the rep for threshold, fixed times for race-pace reps (90 s for 500m, 2 min for 750m, 3 min for 1000m) and 5 min for the 5k-pace reps and half of the preceding rep for pyramids. The 5k-pace session is modelled on the long intervals in the Pete Plan (5×1500, 4×2000, 3k/2.5k/2k, 5 min rest). I read a summary of that page, not the page itself, and the paces are my translation to a 5k split from Paul's law (2k split + 5·log2(5/2), about 6.6 s), not a measured 5k.

TSS = hours × (watts ÷ threshold watts)² × 100, summed over each block at the middle of its split range, as in Cross-Training TSS. Counts towards CTL: the raw TSS times a percentage, 80 by default (a placeholder for all non-running activities there).

Distance for timed blocks is the time divided by the middle split. Concept2 ergometer only. Not adjusted for drag factor, rate or fatigue.
