Notes

  • Reps. Reps are run by time, with the book's rep counts: 1000m = 3 min at race(16), 1600m = 6 min at race(21), 2000m = 8 min at race(30), 3000m = 10 min and 3200m = 12 min at race(32), 5000m = 15 to 18 min at 1.5% faster than marathon pace. Reps are set from current fitness, not goal pace.
  • Recoveries. Walked at 6 km/h: 60 s after 1000m, 75 s after 1600m, 90 s after 2000m and 3000m, 120 s after 3200m and 180 s after 5000m reps. They count for time and TSS but not distance.
  • Warm-up and cooldown. 10 min each at the easy cap. Races use 10 min, but 5km and 10km races add a 20 min warm-up with 2 × 2 min at half-marathon pace and 4 strides. Easy running is at 65% of 5km speed, as a cap.
  • Marathon pace. The Race Prediction Calculator's marathon (70 miles a week). Week 13's 10km and week 14's 3 × 10 min are at that pace, and week 14's 3 × 27.5 min is progressive at 92%, 93% and 100% of marathon speed.
  • Load. TSS = hours × (threshold pace ÷ pace)² × 100, with threshold as race(16). CTL has a 42-day and ATL a 7-day time constant. Start CTL, if blank, is week 1's TSS divided by 7. The marathon is not scored, so week 15 is Monday to Saturday.
