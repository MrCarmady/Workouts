Hard Workout Predictor

Predict a race from a hard interval session, such as 6×1k at 5k effort with short rest.

Workout

Times as mm:ss, or seconds if over 60. If you enter several times, the number of reps is taken from them. A single value is read as the average rep time and uses the Reps box. Enter the pace in /km instead by adding "/km" (for example 3:20/km).

Anchors used

How it works

  • Anchors. Each standard rep length is tied to a race pace at RPE 8–8.5 with its standard rest. The pace for your rep length is interpolated between anchors, then run backwards to find the 5km pace that fits. The anchors are the author's, from experience, not from a published source.
  • Volume. Each anchor has a typical rep count. Pace is scaled by (typical total work / your total work) to the power 0.06, the same exponent as the race model. Rep counts are limited to the supported range for each rep length (my placeholders).
  • Range. Each anchor is given a band of 1% in pace (5000m: marathon pace to 2% faster). That band is my placeholder.
  • Not adjusted. Rest, RPE, heat, surface, wind and hills are not adjusted for. The tool only warns when rest or RPE is far from the anchor.
  • Race model. Riegel 1.06, with a short-distance adjustment below 5km and a marathon offset that assumes 70 miles a week. These are my estimates, not validated.
