Notes

  • Plan. Sessions and distances are as printed in the book and are not scaled. Weeks are labelled by weeks to goal, as in the book. Monday is rest or cross-training throughout.
  • Paces. Recovery is 60% and general aerobic 70% of 5km speed. Medium-long and long runs are at the chosen percentage of marathon-pace speed (80 to 90%). Marathon pace is the Race Prediction Calculator's marathon (70 miles a week). LT is race(21), and VO2max reps are at race(5) with jogs of 70% of rep time at recovery pace. Everything is set from current fitness, not goal pace.
  • Splits. The distance not covered by the LT, marathon-pace or rep work is split evenly into warm-up and cooldown at general aerobic pace. Rep jogs count towards the day's distance.
  • Tune-up races. The book gives a range of total miles. The page uses the middle of that range, with the chosen race distance (week 2 is capped at 10km) scored at race pace and the rest as easy warm-up and cooldown.
  • Strides and hill sprints. Not scored separately. Counted as easy running, they would add roughly 3 to 4 TSS for 8 to 10 strides.
  • Load. TSS = hours × (threshold pace ÷ pace)² × 100, with threshold as race(16). CTL has a 42-day and ATL a 7-day time constant. Start CTL, if blank, is week 1's TSS divided by 7. The marathon is not scored, except when recovery weeks are added, where it is scored at marathon pace so that their load starts from the right place.
  • Recovery. The book's post-marathon block, five weeks of 15 to 41 miles, is optional and not part of the main plan.
