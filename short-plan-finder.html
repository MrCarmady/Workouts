Race Plan Finder

Which Daniels, Davis and Pfitzinger 5K to 10K plans fit your current CTL and ATL, judged on form and the ATL to CTL ratio. To read a plan week by week, open it in the Race Plan Viewer. For marathon plans, use the Marathon Plan Finder.

Inputs

Don't know your CTL?

Rough estimate from your recent running. It assumes steady training and a typical TSS per hour for your race time. Easy pace defaults to 65% of 5km speed. If the pace you enter is faster than you really run, the estimate comes out too high; if slower, too low. A CTL from your watch or training log is better.
