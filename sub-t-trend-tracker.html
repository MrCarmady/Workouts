How it works

  • Implied threshold. The pace and rep length are run backwards through the Sub-Threshold Session Generator's rep-to-race-pace table to find your 5km pace. Threshold is the pace for a 16km race at that fitness. The range spans the fast and slow ends of the table for that rep length.
  • Estimate. The median of the last 6 counted sessions within the past 6 weeks. With fewer than 3 in that window, it uses the last 6 counted sessions and is marked stale.
  • Confidence. Counted sessions in the last 6 weeks. Fewer than 3: stale. 3–5: low. 6–9: moderate. 10 or more: good.
  • Trend. Straight-line fit to the counted sessions in the last 12 weeks, shown from 4 sessions spanning 14 days. Pace noise, heat, hills and fatigue are not corrected for.
  • Race results. The race time is run through the race model to a 5km pace (for races under 5km the same slowing as the Session Generator's input is applied). Threshold is the pace for a 16km race at that 5km pace. The comparison uses the sessions logged in the 6 weeks up to the race date, and shows the race time those sessions predicted. A negative gap means the race was faster than the sessions suggested.
