How it works

  • Implied threshold. The pace and rep length are matched to the same rep-to-race-pace table as the Sub-Threshold Session Generator, run backwards: it finds the 5km pace for which that rep length would be paced as entered. Threshold is the pace for a 16km race at that fitness. The range runs from the fast to the slow end of the table for that rep length.
  • Estimate. The median of the last 6 counted sessions within the past 6 weeks. With fewer than 3 in that window, it uses the last 6 counted sessions and is marked stale.
  • Confidence. Counted sessions in the last 6 weeks. Fewer than 3: stale. 3–5: low. 6–9: moderate. 10 or more: good.
  • Trend. Straight-line fit to the counted sessions in the last 12 weeks, shown from 4 sessions spanning 14 days. Pace noise, heat, hills and fatigue are not corrected for.
