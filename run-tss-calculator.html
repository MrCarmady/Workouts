How this is calculated

Each part scores hours × (threshold pace ÷ average pace)² × 100. One hour at threshold pace is 100. The two parts are added. Longer sessions can score more.

If you enter a race instead of a threshold pace, it is converted to a 5km equivalent with Riegel's formula (time × distance ratio ^ 1.06). Results under 5km are slowed by 8s/km × ln(5 ÷ km). Threshold pace is the pace for 16km from that. These are estimates.

Inputs are rejected if the pace is outside 2:00–15:00 per km, or the session exceeds 120 TSS per hour of total time. A short all-out effort with no warm-up can hit that limit.

Recoveries between reps are not counted. To include them, add their minutes to the warm-up and cool-down line and enter an average pace for the combined time.
