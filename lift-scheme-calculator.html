How this is calculated

The 1RM uses the average of the Brzycki and Epley formulas, applied to reps completed plus reps left in tank. Brzycki: weight × 36 / (37 − reps). Epley: weight × (1 + reps / 30), set to exactly 1 for a single and blended in by 2 reps. Both get less reliable above about 10 reps.

Scheme weights come from a simple fatigue model. The last set is set to your chosen RIR. Each earlier set costs a fixed number of reps of capacity at 3 min rest (0.75 for sets of 3, 1 for sets of 5–6, 1.2 for 8, 1.35–1.6 for 10–12). The rest selector scales that loss: 1.25× at 1 min, 1.1× at 2 min. "By rep range" assumes 3 min for sets of 3–5, 2 min for 6–8 and 1 min for 10–12; picking a fixed time applies it to every scheme. Fresh capacity on set 1 is converted back to % of 1RM with the same two formulas.

Calibrate the fatigue and rest values against your own logs. Squat and deadlift use 1.15× the fatigue.
