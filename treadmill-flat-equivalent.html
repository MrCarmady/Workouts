How this is calculated

Running: ACSM running equation, VO2 = 0.2·S + 0.9·S·G + 3.5 (S in m/min, G as a fraction). Setting the oxygen cost equal to a flat run gives flat speed = belt speed × (1 + 4.5·G).

Walking: ACSM walking equation, VO2 = 0.1·S + 1.8·S·G + 3.5. The same step gives flat-run speed = belt speed × (0.5 + 9·G). Example: 3 mph at 12% is about 28.9 ml/kg/min, the cost of running at about 7:52/km on the flat. ACSM states the walking equation for roughly 3 to 6 km/h; above that, treat the result as less reliable. Handrail use lowers the real cost and is not modelled.

TSS: duration in hours × (threshold pace ÷ flat-equivalent pace)² × 100, the same formula as the running calculators. Blank threshold uses the race result saved on the other pages (16km race pace). This scores the aerobic load. It does not measure the muscle damage or impact of the activity, which differ from running.
