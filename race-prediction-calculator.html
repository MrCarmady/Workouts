How this is calculated

Riegel's formula (time × distance ratio ^ 1.06) is the base, with two adjustments of mine: a result shorter than 5km is slowed by 8 s/km × ln(5 ÷ distance in km) when converted to a 5km equivalent (about 9 s/km for the mile), and predictions for those short distances are sped up by the same amount; and paces beyond the half marathon are slowed by up to 5 s/km at the marathon. A result whose Daniels–Gilbert VDOT falls outside 15 to 85 is rejected as implausible. Treat the times as estimates, not promises.
